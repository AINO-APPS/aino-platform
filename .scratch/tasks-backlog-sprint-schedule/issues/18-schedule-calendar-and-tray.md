# 18: Schedule calendar and tray

**What to build:** The schedule becomes a calendar with month and week views and an unscheduled tray of backlog work. Dragging a task onto a day plans it; dragging it back unschedules it. Each day shows its count and point total so overload is visible. Overdue work is pinned above the calendar. The existing day-grouped list remains available.

**Blocked by:** 05, 08

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Month and week views render planned tasks on their days
- [ ] The unscheduled tray lists backlog tasks without a planned date, ranked
- [ ] Dragging from tray to day sets the planned date; dragging back clears it
- [ ] Each day shows task count and total points
- [ ] Overdue tasks appear in a pinned strip, not inside past days
- [ ] The previous grouped list remains selectable
- [ ] Placing a task on a day does not remove it from the backlog
